More about privacy

What we read: we read sender and subject lines of receipt emails only, locally: the From: and Subject: headers of emails that match receipt searches (purchases, orders, invoices, payments, subscriptions and billing senders, any date, up to 5,000 emails, or 20,000 with "Scan deeper"). Subjects are needed to spot the real shop behind payment services like PayPal, Stripe or Google Play. We never request email bodies, snippets or attachments. Where it goes: nowhere. Your browser talks directly to Google's Gmail API; everything stays in this tab's memory and is gone when you close it. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Access is revoked automatically when the scan finishes. Google's consent screen will say "read your email" because that's the narrowest permission that allows searching.

    FAQ

    Do you see my card details or passwords?

    No. You never type a card number or password here. We only link you to each shop's own page; you sign in there yourself.

    Why can't it delete the cards for me?

    Every shop requires you to sign in to its own site, and no tool can safely do that for you without your password. We cut the job down to: click link, sign in, press delete.

    Some links open the account page, not the cards page

    Some shops don't have a stable direct link (or only manage cards in their app). For those we link the closest page and give you the exact steps. Rows marked account page are those. Shops we don't know yet are marked search: the button searches that shop's own site for its saved cards page.

    What does "Connect Gmail" actually access?

    Google's permission is "read-only Gmail" (there is no narrower one that can search). The app runs a few receipt searches (Gmail's Purchases category, receipt-style subjects, and billing/receipt senders), then asks Google for only the From: and Subject: headers of each match. We read sender and subject lines of receipt emails only, locally: subjects are used in your browser to find the shop behind PayPal, Stripe, Paddle, Apple or Google Play receipts, and to ignore newsletters. Bodies, snippets and attachments are never requested. The token stays in memory, is never saved, and is revoked when the scan ends. You can also check or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

    How do I get access?

    Press Get access and pay once through Stripe. At checkout, enter the Gmail address you want scanned (and a second one if you like, such as an old address). We add those addresses to the app's sign-in list within a few hours and email you your sign-in link. Open that link, connect Gmail and you get the full checklist. This browser remembers your access, so next time just open the site.

    Google says "Access blocked", or I used the wrong Gmail

    While the app is waiting for Google's review, only the Gmail address(es) entered at checkout can sign in. Anyone else sees Google's "Access blocked" screen. Reply to your access email with the right address and we'll switch it.
