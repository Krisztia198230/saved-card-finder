💳 Saved Card Finder: Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026. Plain English first; the details follow.

In short: Saved Card Finder runs entirely in your web browser. We don't have a server that receives your data, we don't store your emails, and we never sell or share anything. If you connect Gmail, the app reads only the sender address of your receipt emails to work out which shops may have your card saved, and forgets it when you close the tab.

1. Data accessed from your Google account (Connect Gmail)

Connecting Gmail is optional. When you click Connect Gmail & scan and approve Google's consent screen, the app receives a short-lived access token for the Google permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly ("Read your email messages and settings"). This is the narrowest Google permission that allows searching your mailbox; Google's metadata-only permission cannot run a search.

With that permission the app does exactly this, from your browser directly to Google's Gmail API:

  1. Three searches for receipt-like emails (Gmail's Purchases category; subjects containing words like receipt, order, invoice, payment, subscription or booking; and senders like billing@ or receipts@), with no date limit, returning up to 5,000 message IDs (20,000 if you choose "Scan deeper").
  2. For each of those messages, a request for only the From: and Subject: headers (e.g. PayPal <service@paypal.co.uk> / "Receipt for your payment to Green Man Gaming"). We request them with Google's format=metadata, metadataHeaders=From, metadataHeaders=Subject and fields=payload/headers options, so Google does not send us the snippet, body, attachments, recipients or any other header.

In plain words: we read sender and subject lines of receipt emails only, locally.

How it is used: in your browser, sender addresses are counted and matched against our built-in list of shops. Subject lines are used only to (a) tell receipts apart from newsletters and (b) find the real shop behind a payment service (for example the shop named in a PayPal, Stripe, Paddle, Apple or Google Play receipt). The result is your checklist of "remove saved card" links, shown only to you. Subjects are never displayed in a list, exported or kept. That is the only use.

Where it goes: nowhere. The token, sender and subject lines exist only in your browser tab's memory. They are not written to your device's storage, not sent to us or any third party, and are discarded when you click Disconnect, clear the page, or close the tab. The access token is revoked with Google automatically as soon as the scan finishes.

Humans: nobody (including us) can see your Gmail data, because it never leaves your browser.

Limited Use disclosure: Saved Card Finder's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to determine creditworthiness, and do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

2. What is saved on your device

To make the checklist usable across visits, the app saves in your browser's local storage: the text you typed in the box, which shops you ticked as done, and whether this browser has access (so the scan opens straight away next time). Gmail data and mailbox files are never saved. You can delete all of it with the "Delete everything this site saved on this device" link at the bottom of the app, or by clearing site data in your browser.

3. Payments and access

Access is a one-off payment handled by Stripe on their own site, under their privacy policy. We never see your card number. At checkout you enter the Gmail address(es) you want scanned (up to 2). We use those addresses only to add them to the app's Google sign-in list (needed while the app is awaiting Google verification) and to email you your sign-in link. We also receive your receipt email address and purchase record for receipts and support. Purchase records are kept for 6 years for UK tax purposes. Ask us at any time to remove your Gmail address(es) from the sign-in list.

4. Hosting and third parties

The site is static files hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security (see GitHub's privacy statement). If you connect Gmail, your browser loads Google's sign-in library from accounts.google.com and talks to Google's APIs; Google's privacy policy applies to that. We use no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or cookies of our own. Links in your checklist open the shops' own websites, which have their own policies.

5. Removing access and deleting data

  • Click Disconnect in the app at any time to stop a scan, revoke the token and clear scanned senders.
  • Review or remove the app's Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete locally saved data as described in section 4. We hold no Gmail data to delete. For purchase records, contact us (see below).

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you (in practice, only purchase records), and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Our lawful basis for processing Gmail data in your browser is your consent; for purchases, performance of a contract.

7. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS with a strict Content Security Policy that allows connections only to Google's sign-in and Gmail API endpoints. Access tokens are short-lived, held in memory only and revoked after use. Report security issues using the contact below.

8. Children

The app is not intended for people under 18.

9. Changes

If we change how Google user data is used, we will update this page and ask for your consent again in the app before any new use.
